Core Game Mechanics

How A Roast Duel Works In 4 Steps

1

Ignite Duel

Creator sets a topic, format (Roast or Compliment), drops the opening burn, and locks GEN stake.

2

Accept Challenge

A rival challenger matches the required wager in GEN and submits their comeback line.

3

AI Jury Deliberation

5 independent GenLayer validator nodes evaluate humor, sharpness, and score both lines 0–10.

4

Consensus Crown

The contract crowns the victor on-chain and transfers the entire 2x pot via internal escrow.
