How AI Consensus Works

Unlike Web2 chatbots, RoastWars uses GenLayer's gl.vm.run_nondet_unsafe equivalence principle. The leader validator prompts an LLM with strict comedy rules, scoring rubrics, and JSON formatting. Validator nodes independently verify agreement on the crowned winner before on-chain state updates.

Zero Gas & Internal Escrow

Stakes are wagered in native GEN and securely pooled in the intelligent contract. Winning pots (2x stake) are programmatically credited to the victor's internal ledger with full withdrawability. Ties split the pot 50/50.
